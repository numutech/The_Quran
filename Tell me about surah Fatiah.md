# Day-1 surah Fatiah

Surah Al-Fatihah, also known as "The Opening," is the first chapter of the Quran, consisting of seven verses that serve as an essential prayer and summary of Islamic faith. It is recited in every unit of the Muslim prayer (Salah) and is considered the greatest surah in the Quran according to authentic Hadith narrated by Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him).

### Overview and Structure

Surah Al-Fatihah was revealed in Mecca and is the opening of the Quran, acting as a preface to the entire book. It is unique because it is a direct dialogue between the worshipper and Allah, where the verses alternate between praise for Allah and supplication from the believer. The surah is divided into two parts: the first half praises Allah's attributes, while the second half seeks guidance and mercy.

### Arabic Text and English Translation

Here is the Arabic text of Surah Al-Fatihah followed by a widely accepted English translation (based on authentic interpretations like those from Sahih International):

- بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
"In the name of Allah, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful."
- الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ
"Praise be to Allah, the Lord of the worlds."
- الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
"The Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful."
- مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ
"Sovereign of the Day of Recompense."
- إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ
"It is You we worship and You we ask for help."
- اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ
"Guide us to the straight path."
- صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا الضَّالِّينَ
"The path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor, not of those who have evoked [Your] anger or of those who are astray."


### Key Themes and Messages

Surah Al-Fatihah conveys the core message of tawhid (the oneness of Allah) and submission to Him, as intended by Allah and emphasized by Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him). It teaches believers to recognize Allah as the Creator, Sustainer, and Judge, while seeking His guidance to follow the straight path of righteousness.

- **Praise and Gratitude**: The surah begins with hamd (praise) to Allah as Rabb al-Alamin (Lord of all worlds), highlighting His mercy (Rahman and Rahim) and authority over the Day of Judgment. This reminds Muslims to be grateful and mindful of accountability, as per Quran 1:1-4.
- **Worship and Dependence**: The verse "It is You we worship and You we ask for help" underscores exclusive devotion to Allah, rejecting any form of polytheism. Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) explained in Sahih Bukhari (Hadith 714) that this surah is a cure and the greatest in the Quran, emphasizing reliance on Allah alone.
- **Guidance and the Straight Path**: The supplication for the "straight path" (Sirat al-Mustaqim) refers to the path of prophets, truthful ones, martyrs, and the righteous, as detailed in Quran interpretations and Hadith. It warns against deviating like those who earned Allah's anger (e.g., those who knew the truth but rejected it) or went astray (e.g., those ignorant of guidance). In Sahih Muslim (Hadith 770), the Prophet described this path as the way of Islam, leading to Paradise.


### Significance in Hadith

Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) highlighted Surah Al-Fatihah's importance in several authentic Hadith:

- It is called Umm al-Kitab (Mother of the Book) because it encapsulates the Quran's essence (Sahih Bukhari, Hadith 4703).
- Reciting it is obligatory in every rak'ah of prayer; without it, the prayer is invalid (Sahih Bukhari, Hadith 756).
- It serves as a ruqyah (healing incantation) for physical and spiritual ailments, as narrated in Sahih Bukhari (Hadith 2272), where companions used it to cure a sting victim.

This surah conveys Allah's invitation to humanity for a relationship based on worship, mercy, and guidance, while Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) taught it as the foundation of daily devotion and a shield against misguidance.
